Sample Topics/Syllabus:

Introduction to Marketing: Definition and scope of marketing, evolution
of marketing concepts, core marketing concepts, importance of marketing
in business

Marketing Environment: Microenvironment and macroenvironment factors,
PESTEL analysis, competitive analysis, environmental scanning

Consumer Behavior: Consumer decision-making process, factors
influencing consumer behavior, psychological and social influences,
buyer personas

Market Segmentation and Targeting: Bases for segmenting markets,
criteria for effective segmentation, targeting strategies, positioning
strategies

Product Strategy: Product levels and classifications, product life
cycle, new product development process, branding and packaging
decisions

Pricing Strategy: Pricing objectives and methods, factors affecting
pricing decisions, pricing strategies for new products, psychological
pricing tactics

Place and Distribution: Marketing channels and intermediaries, channel
design decisions, physical distribution and logistics, retailing and
wholesaling

Promotion and Integrated Marketing Communication: Elements of the
promotion mix, advertising and sales promotion, public relations,
digital marketing and social media

Marketing Research: The marketing research process, primary and
secondary data sources, research methods and tools, applications of
marketing research

Digital Marketing Trends: Search engine optimization, content
marketing, social media marketing strategies, influencer marketing and
analytics
